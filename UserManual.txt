🤟 Gestura User Manual
=====================

Bridging the gap between Deaf and hearing communities through AI.

1. Getting Started
------------------

🔐 Authentication
* Sign Up: Open the app and tap Sign Up. Enter your email and a secure password to create your profile.
* Login: If you already have an account, enter your credentials on the home screen.
* Reset Password: Forgot your password? Tap Reset Password on the login screen to receive a recovery email via Firebase.

2. Core Features
----------------

🎥 ASL Translation (Live Mode)
* Access: Tap the ASL icon in the bottom navigation bar.
* Start Live: Tap the Start Live Translation button to open your camera.
* Signing: Position yourself so your upper body is visible. Gestura will detect your landmarks in real-time.
* Read & Speak: Translated text will appear in the overlay. Tap the Speak button to hear the translation read aloud.
* History: Swipe up on the translation card to save the sentence to your local history.

📁 ASL Translation (Video Upload)
* Select Video: On the ASL tab, scroll through the Recent Videos carousel or tap the upload icon to pick a video from your gallery.
* Analyze: The app will process the video landmarks and display the predicted sign and confidence score.

👤 ASL Avatar (Text-to-Sign)
* Access: Tap the Avatar icon in the bottom navigation.
* Input: Type any word or sentence into the input field.
* Generate: Tap Submit. Our AI will generate a realistic 3D avatar animation of the ASL signs.
* Playback: Use the player controls to loop the animation or adjust the playback speed.

3. Community & Contributions
----------------------------

📈 Contribute Tab
* Help us learn: Tap Contribute to help improve our AI model.
* Record/Pick: Select a gesture you'd like to contribute (e.g., "Hello" or "Thank you").
* Submit: Record a short clip of yourself performing the sign and tap Submit.
* Track Stats: View your contribution milestones (Total, Approved, and Pending) at the top of the screen.

🛠️ Developer Review (Restricted)
* Note: This feature is only available for authorized reviewers.
* Review Queue: View a list of user-contributed gestures.
* Audit: Watch the video and compare it to the predicted label.
* Action: Swipe Right to accept the contribution into the training dataset, or swipe Left to reject it.

4. Settings & Customization
---------------------------

⚙️ App Settings
* Theme: Toggle between Light Mode and Dark Mode for a comfortable viewing experience.
* Model Updates: Tap Check for Updates to download the latest, most accurate AI weights from our servers.
* Account: Update your display name or log out securely from the Settings menu.

5. Troubleshooting
------------------
* Camera Not Working: Ensure you have granted "Camera Permission" in your Android system settings.
* Low Accuracy: Ensure you are in a well-lit environment and your hands are clearly visible to the camera.
* Sync Issues: Check your internet connection if your contribution stats or avatar animations are not loading.
